The Mimic, aka Theater Kid, is a basic packet replay suite.
As with everything else in Adversary, The Mimic has room for improvement.

When fed a pcap file, preferably pcapng, The Mimic will replay whatever packets are inside of the pcapng.
It works via the send() scapy function, so not ethernet.
This is mainly so whatever MACs and artifacts are left in a packet are fixed and not dropped by the AP.
It sends them into the air ruthlessly.

There are many things that can be improved that I shall in the future.
Ran via replay in the Adversary Framework.
