The basic utilities are not worthy of their own single file, so they are here.

exit/quit quits Adversary with a random message generated by the Mersene Twister algorithm, without a traceback, and with a good returncode.

clear prints the \033[H\033[2J to simulate clearing the screen, without calling to the /usr/bin/clear

help/? prints the basic help screen via a python triple quote,
