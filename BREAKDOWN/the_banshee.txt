The Banshee is a basic packet fuzzer and mutation suite.
As with everything else in Adversary, The Banshee is in beta and has room for improvement.

The Banshee is a fuzzer, as previously mentioned, mutates packets in many ways.
It usually turns packets into 'malformed' and such to try and crash devices, or do other various Denial Of Service esque things.
The Banshee supports hopping through channels.
Ran via fuzz in the Adversary Framework.
