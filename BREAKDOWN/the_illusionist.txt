The Illusionist is a beacon flood and spamming tool.
As with everything else in Adversary, it has room for improvement.
The Illusionist spams beacon frames (basically saying 'hey! I am a router! WHY CAN'T YOU LOVE ME!?!?!)
It is what allows devices to 'see' routers, an advertisement.
Devices inherently trust those, and therefore, sometimes, show them up in wifi selection screens.
Even though sometimes, for some reason, they don't show, devices still recieve the influx of beacons.
This means that their batteries often drain faster.

The Illusionist has an option to 'fuzz' the names (randomize them) or spam one SSID.
Devices typically have WiFi scan throttling on, so not all SSIDs may be parsed or shown.
When off, usually via dev options, The Illusionist becomes an absolute monster to devices.

Ran via disconnect-b in the Adversary Framework.
